## Verdict

**The round-2 fix wave is largely real on the live deployment: of the 19 round-2 Critical/High findings, 13 are verified fixed exactly as described, 6 are only partially closed, and none regressed a previously working flow.** The money path is now correct end to end for every product type — 10 real Stripe test payments (one-off packs, discounted packs, two subscriptions, a drop-in lesson, shop orders) were all fulfilled in the app with the right credits, `Transaction` rows, fee split, HQ shop sales and exactly one receipt each; the subscription replay hole (R2-C3) is closed; the cancellation policy now agrees between UI and server in the school's timezone. Cross-tenant isolation stayed clean across 4,042 token × endpoint combinations, and the teacher chat leak (R2-C2) is gone at REST and WebSocket level.

**But the platform is still not launch-ready.** Two new Critical items: the HQ account-takeover fix guarded `PATCH` only — **`PUT /api/hq/team/<id>/` still lets a `team`-only role rewrite a super_admin and promote itself to owner** — and **any teacher can upload an HTML/SVG file through the profile-photo endpoint that is then served from the app's own origin as `text/html`** (stored XSS with the JWT in `localStorage`). Five High items: narrow HQ roles still read, download and delete every student's private document (the R2-H2 fix stopped at `/api/school/`), Stripe Connect onboarding has no role gate and races into orphan accounts, removing an HQ member now deactivates the account irreversibly (a regression of the R2-M19 fix), a co-owner can remove the school founder, and the Stripe webhook still never reaches dev (0 of 3 blocked-return purchases fulfilled — an environment/dashboard item, not code). One round-2 Low fix is not in effect at all: the e-mail copy fixes of PR #110 changed the built-in templates, but dev's database holds edited copies of those templates that take precedence.

Counts: **Critical 2 · High 5 · Medium 16 · Low 14** (consolidated; items found by more than one agent are merged and cross-referenced).

Add an image allow-list (extension + magic bytes, or re-encode with Pillow) in `ModelImageUploadView`/`save_public`, and serve `/media/public/` with `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` + a fixed image `Content-Type` (or from a separate origin). Replace every bare `is_hq()` on data paths (`students/document_views.py`, `catalog/consumers.py`, `chat` conversation create) with `hq_school_godmode()`. Gate `POST /stripe/onboard/` behind the `payments` section / owner-admin and make `start_connect_onboarding` idempotent (`select_for_update`). Make `StudentSelfSerializer` read-only for `school`/`email`/`user`.
2. **Onboarding and ownership (R3-H3, R3-H4, R3-M2, R3-M12).** On re-invite of a deactivated user restore `is_active` and roles (or de-authorize by revoking tokens/membership instead of deactivating the account); protect `School.owner`'s membership from other owners and make owner demotion explicit; propagate `email_sent` in `teachers/resend` and surface it in the HQ school-detail and Team pages; validate invite e-mails with `EmailValidator`.
3. **Money and environment (R3-H5, R3-M6, R3-M9, R3-M11, R3-M14).** Register the dev webhook and prove it with the blocked-return test (or add the reconciliation task); reject a second compensation payment for the same teacher/month (or make it an explicit update) and validate `amount > 0`; add `validate_credit_cost` to `CourseSerializer`; validate `School.timezone` against `zoneinfo.available_timezones()`; re-seed the DB e-mail templates and consider a "reset to built-in" button on the HQ Emails page.
4. **Robustness and product gaps (R3-M4, R3-M5, R3-M8, R3-M3, R3-M15, R3-M16, R3-M7).** Accept a slug in `/api/student/school/` (or resolve it on the register page); enforce `expiry_date` in `_missing_required_document_names()`; apply `ensure_object_body` and id validation across the remaining views; refresh state after activate/deactivate; add the language switcher to the auth pages and the mobile landing; fix the student-profile link in the school inbox; make the two mobile tables scroll.
5. **Polish (Lows).** Credits-low copy and trigger, raw enum badges, the remaining school-panel hardcoded strings and exports, 404 localization, Spanish casing, empty-body creates, case-insensitive login, closure-day modal, anonymous profile/support prompts, HSTS redeploy, data-hygiene cleanup.
