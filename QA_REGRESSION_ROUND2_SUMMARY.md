## Verdict

**Round 1's Critical/High fixes are genuinely in place on the live deployment — 12 of 14 verified fixed exactly as described — but the platform is still not launch-ready.** Two of the round-1 fixes did not close the money path in effect (the Stripe webhook is still never processed on dev, so shop orders still have no automatic fulfilment), one fix introduced a real regression (school Settings can no longer be saved), and the new subscription activation is not idempotent (a student can refill a subscription to full credits by reopening the success URL). Independently of the purchase pipeline, this round found four new authorization defects, two of them severe: an HQ role holding only the `team` permission can **take over an owner/super_admin account**, and **any teacher can read, answer and delete every private student↔school chat** at their school (found independently by two agents). Cross-tenant isolation between schools is otherwise solid (2,133 combinations, zero cross-tenant reads), the credit/cancellation engine is correct (with one timezone caveat), and the systemic i18n raw-key regression of earlier rounds is absent everywhere.

Counts: **Critical 4 · High 15 · Medium 20 · Low 14** (consolidated; duplicates found by more than one agent are merged and cross-referenced).

### R2-C1 — Owner/super_admin takeover by a `team`-only HQ role (HQ-R2-01)
- **Where:** `backend/accounts/hq_views.py` `HQMemberViewSet.partial_update()` — only `sub_role` is hierarchy-guarded; `email`, `name`, `phone`, `active` are written for any target. `email` is the login credential (synced to `User.email`).
- **Repro (live, reverted within the test):** create custom role "QA R2 Team Coordinator" `[dashboard, team]`, assign it to `qa.hq.tech_support`. As that user: `PATCH /api/hq/team/<qa.hq.super_admin id>/ {"email":"qa-r2-hq-takeover@uberip.com"}` → **200**. Login with the old e-mail → 401; with the new e-mail + old password → 200. ## Methodology, decisions and hazards

- **Tooling.** No browser-automation MCP tool was available in this session, so Playwright 1.55 with Chromium (headless shell) was installed in the session scratchpad and driven from Node scripts; a shared helper gave each agent a persistent browser context of its own (`launchPersistentContext` per agent name), a UI-login helper, a JWT-injection helper (same localStorage keys the app uses), `whoAmI()` (decode the JWT before trusting a page) and screenshot/text-dump helpers. Chromium in Playwright cannot decode H.264, so video playback was verified with a public WebM plus the CSP header. Stripe's hosted Connect onboarding blocked automation with an hCaptcha at step 1 (School B), so a second Stripe-connected school could not be produced; all real payments ran on QA Test School (connected in round 1).
- **E-mail.** The Gmail connector needs re-authorization and was not usable. Disposable inboxes (mail.tm, domain `uberip.com`) were used for every new account; the platform delivered to them within 2–12 s. 90+ e-mails were verified for delivery, subject, per-recipient locale and link validity (invites, welcome, password reset, purchase receipts, booking confirmed/cancelled, credits low, no-show, lesson cancelled, account deleted, new-school HQ notification, template test-sends in 5 locales). School-side copies (`school.new_booking` etc.) go to the school's contact address and were only verifiable for the round-2 schools, whose contact address is a disposable inbox (18 received on School C).
- **Tenancy.** The coordinator created one fresh school per agent through the real HQ flow (`POST /api/hq/schools/` → `resend-invite` → e-mail → `/setup-account` → `complete-invite`): QA R2 School B (School agent), C (Teacher agent), E1 + E2 (cross-cutting agent). QA Test School was reserved for the Student/payments agent; the HQ agent created and deleted its own "QA R2 School A". The three production-like schools were read-only. Two cross-agent touches happened and are disclosed: the HQ agent posted and deleted a probe message in an E2 chat while proving R2-H2, and it accidentally deleted two global Library seed rows (re-created, R2-L14). The cross-cutting agent's own destructive probes deleted several of its E1 fixtures mid-run (rebuilt). The Student agent deleted a stray message it had posted into a round-1 conversation.
- **Coordinator intervention on the environment.** `enabled.team_invite` was `false` at 09:58 UTC (R2-H15). It was set to `true` at 10:00 UTC through the real HQ endpoint so invite flows could be tested, and **restored to `false` at ~12:30 UTC** (environment left as found; verified `GET /api/hq/email-settings/`). Consequence for the team: invites on dev are silently dropped again until someone decides otherwise — see Action items.
- **Shared-IP throttles.** All agents and the coordinator shared one client IP; dev throttles registration and password reset at 5/hour and login at 10/minute per IP. This produced 429s for three agents, cost roughly 40 minutes of waiting, and was handled with a shared ledger (each agent logged every registration/reset and paced to the window) and cached tokens with `/api/auth/refresh/` instead of re-logins. It is recorded once as R2-M15 (product side: raw English 429 text in the localized form) and otherwise treated as an environment effect. Registrations this round: 10 (coordinator 1, Student 2, School 2, Teacher 2, cross-cutting 2, HQ 0 + 1 via invite).
- **Django Admin** was excluded on every agent's instructions (only `/admin/login/` reachability was noted).
- **Agent stall.** The Student agent's session hit a 10-minute inactivity watchdog after it had finished writing its report; it was resumed for its final summary. Nothing was re-run.
- **Severity calls by the coordinator** that differ from an agent: R2-M1 (systemic 500s) downgraded from High to Medium (DEBUG off, no data impact, no auth bypass); R2-H2 kept at High rather than Critical because it requires an authenticated HQ account (internal staff), though it is destructive across all tenants.
